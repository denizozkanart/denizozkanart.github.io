In short: Fuse Rush does not collect, sell or share your personal data.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking in the current version.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Fuse Rush: Rope Puzzle ("the game") is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad made by Kyro Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal details, and it does not use device or advertising identifiers.

Information stored on your device

To remember where you are, the game saves your progress on your own device: the level you reached, coins, lives, boosters and settings such as sound, music and vibration. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

Permissions

The game does not request access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location. It uses the device's vibration for touch feedback, which you can switch off in the game's settings.

Third parties

The current version contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services. Apple processes downloads, and any purchases, under Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you test a pre-release version through TestFlight, Apple may share with us the crash reports and feedback you choose to send, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. We use these only to fix problems in the game.

Children

The game is made for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (EU and UK), Türkiye's KVKK (Law No. 6698) and the CCPA (California) give you rights over your personal data, including access and deletion. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds advertising, analytics or in-app purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released, update the privacy details on the App Store, and ask for your permission where the law or Apple requires it. The date at the top shows when the policy last changed.
